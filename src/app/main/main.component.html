✳ About me

Hi, I'm Mahendra.

I'm a frontend developer in Hyderabad with a little over four years of experience. Most of it has gone into healthcare products: apps for patients, tools for support teams, and dashboards for the companies that buy the plans.

Right now I'm at Tech Navitas, working on HEBA, a benefits administration platform for a US client. Before that I spent three years at Flip Health, where I led frontend work on apps used by more than 50,000 people.

I like work you can measure. At Flip Health I took 30% off the first load, and our test suites brought regression bugs down by 40%. I also mentored three junior developers, which taught me as much as it taught them.

I mostly write Angular and React with TypeScript, and I'm comfortable writing the Node.js and Express API behind a feature, so I can see it through from the screen to the database.

If that sounds like what your team needs, let's talk.
